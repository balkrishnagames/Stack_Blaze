Delete Account & Data

Last updated: September 2026

Stack Blaze does not use accounts or sign-in — there is nothing to register and nothing stored on our servers, because we do not operate a backend server. All of your game data (score, coins, unlocked themes, settings) is saved locally on your own device only.

How to delete your data

Option 1 — Clear app storage:
Android Settings → Apps → Stack Blaze → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This permanently erases all local game data.

Option 2 — Uninstall the App:
Uninstalling Stack Blaze removes all of its locally stored data from your device automatically.

Advertising Data

The App shows ads via Google AdMob, which may associate your device's Advertising ID with ad activity. To reset or opt out of ad personalization, go to your device's Settings → Google → Ads, and use "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

Still need help?

If you believe any data related to your use of Stack Blaze needs to be removed and the steps above don't cover it, email us at balkrishnagames@gmail.com and we'll assist you.
