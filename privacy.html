Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Balkrishna Games ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Stack Blaze mobile application ("App"). We built Stack Blaze to be playable without creating an account, and we collect as little data as possible.

1. No Account, No Personal Sign-Up Data

Stack Blaze does not require you to register, sign in, or provide your name, email, or phone number to play. There is no account system.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

Your score, coins, unlocked themes, and settings are saved locally on your device (via local storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any of our servers, because we do not operate a backend server.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — displays in-app advertisements and may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location, and app activity to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Review — used to prompt for App ratings via Google Play's standard review dialog. Governed by Google Play's own privacy practices.
  • Google Play Services — used for core app functionality and distribution.

4. Notifications

With your permission, the App schedules local reminder notifications (e.g. "come back and play") directly on your device. These notifications are generated on-device and are not based on any personal data we collect.

5. Children's Privacy

Stack Blaze is rated for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown in the App follows Google Play's Families and AdMob policies for age-appropriate content.

6. Data Deletion

Since all gameplay data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. See our Delete Account & Data page for details.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at balkrishnagames@gmail.com.
